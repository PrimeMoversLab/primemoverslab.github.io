Energy

Clean, cheap, abundant energy is the foundation of everything else. Solar costs have collapsed, fusion has crossed ignition in the lab, and AI data centers have turned firm, carbon-free power into the world’s most urgent buildout. The next decade is about fission and fusion at scale, longer-duration storage, and denser batteries.

By 2050: energy abundant enough to power billions of lives, with almost no carbon.

Infrastructure

Civilization runs on systems that move power, water, and information. LEO broadband now reaches nearly every corner of Earth, but the grid must be rebuilt for electrification and AI load, and water stress keeps rising. Breakthroughs in materials, wireless, and treatment will decide how fast we can build.

By 2050: resilient grids, clean water, and gigabit connectivity for everyone.

Thought papers: Electricity Grid · LEO SatCom · Water Treatment

Transportation

Robotaxis carry paying riders in major US cities, driverless trucks haul freight, and air taxis and supersonic jets are in flight test. Launch keeps getting cheaper, opening a cislunar economy. The challenge now is certification, cost, and scale.

By 2050: autonomous, electric, and supersonic travel on Earth, with a permanent presence on the Moon.

Manufacturing

By 2050: abundant goods made by intelligent machines, cleanly and close to home.

Agriculture

Food demand could rise as much as 70% while farming must use less land, water, and fertilizer. Cultivated meat reached US restaurants in 2023, precision tools are mainstream, and engineered microbes are replacing synthetic nitrogen. Cost parity with conventional food is the next hurdle.

By 2050: more food for everyone, on less land, with far less suffering.

By 2050: decades of added healthy life, and minds that are stronger and more connected.
